Privacy Policy

Closed Alpha Reminder

Effective date: November 12, 2026

The short version

Your list stays on your phoneNo accounts or sign-inNo internet accessNo ads, analytics or tracking

Closed Alpha Reminder keeps track of the Google Play closed tests you’ve joined as a tester and reminds you to open each app every day. I don’t collect your information. The app doesn’t ask Android for internet access, so it can’t send your list or anything else to me or to any server.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • The apps you’re testing: app name, package name, the links you saved (tester group, opt-in and Play Store links), the date you joined, the days you opened each app, and whether the developer said they’re done
  • Developer details you type or that come from a post you share in: the developer’s name, email address and any other contact (such as a Discord or Reddit name)
  • Your notes for each developer, and the original post text if you added an app with Share
  • Your tester groups: their names, links and your notes about them, plus which group you last used for posts shared from each app (such as Discord), so it can be filled in next time
  • Your settings: reminder times, light or dark mode, color palette, text size and splash screen choices

Developer names and email addresses belong to other people. They stay on your phone with the rest of your list and are never sent anywhere by the app.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your daily reminder to open the apps you’re testing. Reminders are created on your phone; nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings or in Android’s settings.
  • Run at startup: so your reminders are set up again after your phone restarts.
  • Seeing your installed apps: the app checks whether each app you’re testing is installed, so it can open it with the Open button, show its icon, and warn you if it was uninstalled. This check happens only on your phone, and the app doesn’t keep or share a list of your installed apps.

The app doesn’t use your camera, contacts, location, photos, files or microphone.

Sharing posts into the app

You can share a post (for example from Discord, Reddit, a Google Group or an email) into Closed Alpha Reminder. The app reads only the text you share, and only when you do it, to fill in the links, email address and group. Android may also tell the app which app the post came from (such as Discord); that’s used only to remember your group for next time. The app is saved only after you check the form and tap Save.

Importing from Google Groups

To import your Google Groups, you copy the text of your “My groups” page in your own browser and paste it into the app. The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, and only reads the text to find group names and links. It never signs in to Google, never sees your Google password, and never reads your clipboard on its own.

Sending notes and opening links

If you tap Email, Share or Copy, the app passes your notes to the app you choose (such as your email app), or puts them on your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you do this yourself, and once it’s sent, that app’s own privacy practices apply.

Links you save (tester groups, opt-in pages, Play Store pages) open in your browser or the Google Play app, which have their own privacy practices. Joining a tester group or opting in to a test is between you, Google and that app’s developer.

Android’s built-in backup

Android may include the app’s data (your list, notes, groups and settings) in your phone’s own Google backup, so it can come back if you move to a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

Links to other sites

The About section has an optional Venmo link for donations, which opens in your web browser or the Venmo app. Venmo has its own privacy practices. The app doesn’t know whether you donate.

Google Play

The app is installed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy. I don’t receive that information in a form that identifies you.

Deleting your data

You can delete any app or group in Closed Alpha Reminder at any time, and edit or clear your notes. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Notes you emailed or shared stay wherever you sent them.

Children

The app isn’t directed to children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and its effective date.
