Privacy Policy

Drills & Pieces: The Hobby & Craft Timer

Effective date: November 13, 2026

The short version

Your data stays on your phoneNo accounts or sign-inNo ads, analytics or tracking

Drills & Pieces is a hobby tracker for diamond art, puzzles and Lego projects. I don’t collect your information, and the app doesn’t send it to me or to any server.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • Project details, such as title, type, status, dates, cost, notes, purchase link, theme, rating and drill colors
  • Work-session times from the timer
  • Your drill stock
  • Photos you add, which are copied into the app so they keep working even if you move or delete the original
  • Your settings, such as theme, palette and backup options

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools.

Photos

When you add a photo, you choose it with Android’s own photo picker, and the app only gets the photos you pick. The app doesn’t use your camera.

Backups you create

  • Export and import: when you export a backup, the app saves a file (JSON, or ZIP with photos) to a place you choose. When you import, the app reads only the file you pick.
  • Automatic export (off unless you turn it on): if you turn it on, the app saves backup files on the schedule you choose to a folder you pick, and keeps the number of recent backups you set. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • Wherever you save a backup (your phone, an SD card or a cloud storage app), that place’s own privacy practices apply.

Android’s built-in backup

Android may include the app’s data in your phone’s own Google backup, so it can come back if you move to a new phone. To keep that backup small, photos are left out of cloud backup; projects, sessions, drill stock and settings may be included. That backup is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your phone’s settings. When you transfer data directly from an old phone to a new one, photos are included.

Notifications

The app asks permission to show notifications. It uses them only to tell you if an automatic backup export fails. You can say no, or turn notifications off later, and the app still works.

Sharing and copying

If you tap Share, Text project or Copy, the app passes the text (and the cover photo, if you include it) to the app you choose, or puts it on your clipboard. Nothing is shared unless you do this yourself.

Home screen widget

If you add the widget, it shows the selected project’s title, time and cover photo on your home screen, so anyone who can see your screen can see them. The widget’s start/stop button works the same as the timer inside the app.

Links to other sites

The app has links that open in your web browser: purchase links you add, this privacy policy, Discord and Venmo. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Google Play

The app is installed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy. I don’t receive that information in a form that identifies you.

Deleting your data

You can delete projects, photos, sessions and drill colors inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

The app isn’t directed to children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and its effective date. The same policy is also published at sites.google.com/view/drillspiecesapp.

Feedback and email

The app has a Feedback option in Settings & Options. When you send feedback, the app opens your own email app with the message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. The app itself doesn’t send anything.

If you send it, I receive your email address, your message, and, only if you leave “Include app and phone details” on, the app version, your Android version and your phone model. Your projects, photos, notes and drill stock are never included.

You can also choose Send anonymously. That opens my “App Feedback” Google Form in your web browser, with your message, the app name and feedback type filled in, plus the app version and phone model only if that switch is on. You review it and tap Submit yourself. The form doesn’t ask for your name, and your email is optional; leave it blank and I can’t tell who sent it. Google hosts the form under its own privacy policy.

I use feedback only to reply to you and to improve the app. I never sell or share it, unless required by law. You can ask me to delete your emails at any time.
