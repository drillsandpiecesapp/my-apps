Privacy Policy

PXL MMO Companion

Effective date: November 13, 2026

The short version

What you enter stays on your phoneNo accounts or sign-inFree, with ads by Google AdMobNo analytics or tracking of our own

PXL MMO Companion is free and shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. Everything you type into the app stays on your phone, and I never receive it. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. To show and measure ads, Google’s ad service collects some information from your device, explained below under Ads.

PXL MMO Companion is a free, fan-made companion app for the game PXL MMO, made by HokuKai330 with the game developer’s permission. It is not an official product of the game or its developer. This policy covers the Android app with the package name com.hokukai330.pxlmmocompanion.

What the app stores on your phone

The app saves your choices on your device so it can remember them between uses. This information is never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Your in-game levels that you type in (fishing, farming, cooking) and your rod tier
  • Fish you star, items on your wishlist and players you follow on the leaderboards (by their public in-game name)
  • Farm timers you start
  • Your theme, layout and sorting choices
  • A short-term copy of game data, such as the latest weather and leaderboards, so pages load quickly

The app does not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details.

Ads

A banner ad at the bottom of the screen is provided by Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • Your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • Information about your device and the app, such as the model, Android version and app version
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps, plus diagnostic information

This information goes to Google, not to me, and Google handles it under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that show its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the app shows Google’s consent message before any ad loads, so you can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer any time in Settings > About > Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads, depending on the phone).

The app does not tell Google anything you type into it, such as your levels, starred fish or farm timers.

How the app uses the internet

Apart from ads, the app only downloads public game information: fish, crops, items, recipes, enemies, weather and leaderboards. It gets this from the game’s public data service at pxlmmo.roman.tools, which the game’s developer runs. The app sends no personal information in these requests. Like any website, that server can see basic connection details such as your IP address, under its own practices.

The app does not upload anything you enter. For example, players you follow are matched on your phone, not sent to the server.

The Settings page has an optional link to the developer’s Venmo page. Tapping it opens Venmo in your browser or the Venmo app, where Venmo’s own privacy policy applies. The app never sees or handles any payment.

Notifications

The app can remind you when a starred fish is biting or a farm timer is done. These reminders are created on your phone. You can allow or turn off notifications at any time in Android settings, and the app works fully without them.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app’s saved settings in that backup. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and we cannot see it. You can control it in your phone’s backup settings.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13, and it is not designed for or marketed to children. Ads in the app are not intended for children.

Deleting your data

Everything you enter is on your phone. To delete it, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. I never receive it, so there is nothing for me to delete. To delete what Google’s ad service has linked to your advertising ID, delete or reset the ID in your phone’s settings; Google’s own controls are at myadcenter.google.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, for example through a future optional player sign-in or a different ad provider, this policy will be updated before that change is released. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Feedback and email

The Send feedback button in Settings > About opens your own email app with a draft to hokukai330@gmail.com. The draft includes the app version and your Android version so a bug can be matched to the right build. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, and you can edit or delete anything in the draft first.

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use it only to reply to you and to improve the app. I never sell or share it, unless required by law. You can ask me to delete your emails at any time.
