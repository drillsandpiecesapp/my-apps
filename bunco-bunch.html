Everything you need for Bunco night

Rule sets

Play it your way
  • Classic Party Bunco for big groups
  • Low / High Bunco tracks high and low scores across tables
  • Small Group for 2 to 4 players
  • House Rules with your own points for Buncos and Mini-Buncos

Game setup

Ready in seconds
  • 2 to 40 players, 12 by default
  • Edit every player’s name
  • Set the buy-in and the total pot is worked out for you
  • Start a new game and keep the names, or reset everything

Prizes

Classic with 4 categories, Inclusive with 6, or your own dollar amounts
Most WinsMost LossesMost BuncosLast Bunco / Traveler50/50Booby PrizeMost Mini-Buncos
  • Set an exact amount for each prize and check it against the total pot as you go
  • Any leftover custom pot goes to the game leaders automatically

Live score sheet

Real-time tracking
  • Log Wins, Losses, Buncos, Mini-Buncos and the Traveler token for every player
  • Current leaders are highlighted by wins and Buncos
  • Who Wins What? shows the qualifying winners, each payout, and every player’s total earnings

Ties and roll-offs

No more arguing
  • Split a prize evenly among tied players
  • Or turn on roll-off mode and pick the roll-off winner right on the score sheet

History and standings

Keep the whole season
  • Every finished night is saved automatically when you start a new game, with scores, prize winners and payouts
  • Standings add up each player’s wins, Buncos and money won for this year or all time
  • Share a night’s results to your group chat in one tap
  • Missed a night? Add it to your History from the results text someone shared

Rules guide

How to install

Bunco Bunch is installed through Google Play.

  1. Open the Google Play page. Tap “Get it on Google Play” on your Android phone, or search Google Play for Bunco Bunch.
  2. Install it. Tap Install.
  3. Open the app. Tap Open, or find Bunco Bunch in your app list.
Updating: Google Play updates the app for you. Your current game and History stay on your phone.

Free, with no ads

Bunco Bunch costs nothing to download and never shows ads. Donations are appreciated but never required.
