Drills & Pieces

Keep every diamond art, puzzle and Lego project in one place, with timers, progress and notes.

Download for AndroidJoin the Discord

Free to download. No ads. Android only.

What it does

  • Track diamond art, puzzle and Lego projects, with drills for diamond art and pieces for puzzles and Legos.
  • Time every session and see your total time across all crafts.
  • Scan a barcode to fill in details when the item is in the database. If nothing is found, the item isn’t listed, and you can enter it by hand.
  • Save where you bought something, what it cost, and your own notes.
  • Browse the DMC color list for diamond art.
  • Share a project by text message, including its cover image.
  • Light and dark themes, with optional automatic backups to a folder you choose.

How to install

This app isn’t on Google Play, so Android asks you to allow the install. That warning is normal for apps downloaded outside the store.

  1. Download the file. Tap “Download for Android” on your phone. If the browser warns that this file type can be harmful, choose to keep it.
  2. Open it. Find the file in your notifications or the Downloads app and tap it.
  3. Allow the install. If Android says installs from this source are blocked, tap Settings, turn on “Allow from this source,” then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open the app. Tap Open, or find Drills & Pieces in your app list.
Updating: download the newest file and install it over the old one. Your projects stay as they are. You can back up first with Export now in the app’s settings.
