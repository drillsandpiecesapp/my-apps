How to install

Drills & Pieces is installed through Google Play. You join the testing group first, then Google Play gives you access.

  1. Join the group. Tap “Download for Android” on your phone and join the Google Group with the Google account you use on Google Play.
  2. Become a tester. Follow the link in the group’s welcome message to opt in to testing.
  3. Install from Google Play. Open the Google Play page from that link and tap Install.
  4. Open the app. Tap Open, or find Drills & Pieces in your app list.
Updating: Google Play updates the app for you. Your projects stay as they are, and you can back up first with Export now in the app’s settings.
