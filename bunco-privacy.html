Privacy Policy

Bunco Bunch Tally Sheet

Effective date: November 13, 2026

The short version

Your games stay on your phoneNo accounts or sign-inNo internet accessNo ads, analytics or tracking

Bunco Bunch is a score sheet for Bunco night. I don’t collect your information. The app doesn’t ask Android for internet access, so it can’t send your games or anything else to me or to any server.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • The current game: rule set, number of players, player names, buy-in, prize settings, tie rules and scores (wins, losses, Buncos and Mini-Buncos)
  • Saved nights in History: the date, scores, prize winners and payouts from each finished game, and any nights you add from shared text
  • Your settings, such as light or dark mode and color palette

Player names are whatever you type. They might be your friends’ names, and they stay on your phone with the rest of the game.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools. It doesn’t ask for any permissions, so it can’t use your camera, contacts, location, photos or files.

Sharing results

If you tap Share, the app writes the night’s results as text (date, prize winners, payouts, player names and scores) and passes it to the app you choose, such as your messaging app. Nothing is shared unless you do this yourself. Once it’s sent, that app’s own privacy practices apply.

Adding a night from text

You can add a night you missed by sharing the results text into Bunco Bunch, or by pasting it in History. The app reads only the text you share or paste, and only when you do it. It never reads your clipboard on its own. The night is saved only after you check the preview and tap Save.

Android’s built-in backup

Android may include the app’s data (your current game, History and settings) in your phone’s own Google backup, so it can come back if you move to a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

Links to other sites

The About screen has an optional Venmo link for donations, which opens in your web browser or the Venmo app. Venmo has its own privacy practices. The app doesn’t know whether you donate.

Google Play

The app is installed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy. I don’t receive that information in a form that identifies you.

Deleting your data

You can delete saved nights in History, and reset the current game on the Setup screen, at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Results you shared stay wherever you sent them.

Children

The app isn’t directed to children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and its effective date.

Feedback and email

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use it only to reply to you and to improve the app. I never sell or share it, unless required by law. You can ask me to delete your emails at any time.
