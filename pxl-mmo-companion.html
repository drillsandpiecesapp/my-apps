PXL MMO Companion

Your pocket guide to PXL MMO: what time it is in game, which fish are biting, when your crops are ready and what you can cook, craft and fight next.

Game clock and weather

Right on the home screen
  • The in-game time, day or night, and the current weather
  • How long until dusk and other coming changes
  • How many fish are biting for you right now

Fishing

Know what’s biting
  • Fish biting now, with your catch chance
  • Fish coming later today, like ones that only bite at dusk or in rain
  • Star your favorites and get a reminder when they’re biting

Farm

Never miss a harvest
  • Timers for every crop, with harvest and water-again countdowns
  • Reminders when crops are ready or need water
  • Track your farming level and the plots in use
  • Work out your harvest bonus from tools, hats, drinks and compost

Cooking and crafting

What can I make?
  • Recipes you can make at your level, and the ones still locked
  • The burn chance for each dish
  • Sort by level, what heals most or what gives the most XP

Items

Gear and rarities
  • Browse gear from Rare to Legendary
  • Keep a wishlist of items you’re after

Combat and levels

Plan your build
  • Warrior, Ranger and Cleric classes, with each ability’s damage, cooldown and range
  • Level planner and skill XP calculator
  • Every skill, from Attack and Strength to Magic

Map

Find your way
  • All 5 areas, with camps, keepers, the farm, PvP and safe zones
  • Enemy camps fade when they’re down

Leaderboards

See who’s on top
  • Browse the rankings and follow players by their in-game name

Make it yours

Themes and layout
  • Choose your theme, layout and sorting
  • Seasonal touches throughout the year
