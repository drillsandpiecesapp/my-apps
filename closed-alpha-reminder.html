Made for app testers

Today at a glance

Your daily checklist
  • See how many of your test apps you’ve opened today, with a progress bar
  • Open any app straight from the list, or mark it as opened yourself
  • Each app shows its developer, where you found it and which day of the test you’re on
  • Filter the list by tester group

Daily reminders

Never miss a day
  • Reminders at the times you choose, like morning and evening
  • Reminders are set up again after your phone restarts

Testing progress

Know where you stand
  • Day count, join date and how many days you opened the app
  • Clear notice once you pass the 14-day minimum
  • “Developer says they’re done” stops the reminders for that app

How to join, saved for you

Every link in one place
  • Keep the tester group, opt-in and install links for each app, with Open and Copy buttons
  • Reinstall in one tap if you ever uninstall a test app
  • A warning if a test app is no longer installed

Add apps from a post

Share it in
  • Share a post from Discord, Reddit, a Google Group or email into the app
  • The links, email and group are filled in for you. Check them and tap Save

Developer details and notes

Feedback made easy
  • Save the developer’s name, email and other contact
  • Write notes as you test, then email, share or copy them to the developer

Tester groups

Discord servers, subreddits and Google Groups
  • See how many apps you’re testing from each group, how many are finished and how many aren’t opened today
  • Keep each group’s rules in a note, like “test 3 apps before posting yours”
  • Import your Google Groups by pasting your “My groups” page

Make it yours

Themes and comfort
  • System default, Light or Dark mode, and a choice of color palettes
  • Large text option, on top of your phone’s text size
  • Splash screen with seasonal looks, or turn it off

Your privacy

No personal data collectedNo login neededNo internet accessNo ads or tracking

Your list, notes and developer details stay on your phone. The app doesn’t ask for internet access, so it can’t send anything anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
